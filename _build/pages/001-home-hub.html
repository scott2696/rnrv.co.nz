What is changing for online gambling NZ

1 December 2026 is the date to know

The Online Casino Gambling Act 2026 came into force on 1 May 2026 and the Department of Internal Affairs is awarding 15 online casino licences. From 1 December 2026, any operator without a licence or a pending application must stop serving New Zealanders.

Practically, that means some sites Kiwis use today will exit the market. If you hold a balance anywhere, withdraw it rather than leaving it, and complete your verification now so a withdrawal cannot stall at the worst moment.
