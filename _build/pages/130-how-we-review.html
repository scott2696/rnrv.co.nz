The testing process, step by step

Before any operator appears anywhere on RNRV, it goes through this. It takes roughly three weeks.

  1. Register as an ordinary customer

    A reviewer opens an account in their own name, from a New Zealand IP address, with no affiliate account, no VIP contact and no advance notice to the operator. We record every friction point in the sign-up flow, because that is the experience a reader will have.

  2. Complete KYC

    Photo ID, proof of address, proof of payment method, submitted on day one. We record how long verification takes and how clearly the requirements are communicated, because that is the single largest determinant of how fast a first withdrawal goes.

  3. Deposit real New Zealand dollars

    Our own money, on at least two different payment rails where the operator supports more than one. We record deposit speed, any currency conversion applied, and whether the advertised minimum matches the actual minimum.

  4. Play the catalogue

    A minimum of 200 real-money pokie spins or 100 live dealer hands. During this we sample 60 titles from the lobby and read each one's RTP from its in-game information panel — not from the studio's documentation, because operators deploy different configurations.

  5. Read the complete terms

    Our editor reads the full terms and conditions and the bonus policy, recording the wagering multiple, what it applies to, the maximum bet during wagering, game weighting, expiry, maximum conversion, withdrawal caps and fees. Not the summary on the promotions tile — the actual terms.

  6. Test support with a real question

    A live chat asking something that requires an actual answer, usually about withdrawal caps or bonus weighting. We record response time and whether the agent answered or deflected. Transcripts are kept.

  7. Request a withdrawal and time it

    To the minute, with two separate timestamps: when the operator approved, and when funds were available. We repeat this at different times of day and on different rails. Ninety-four such withdrawals underpin the current site.

  8. Score, fact-check, publish

    The reviewer scores against the weights below. A second person fact-checks every claim. The page is published with a byline and a review date. No operator sees it first.

Scoring weights

The single rating shown against each operator is produced from these six components.

RNRV scoring model. Ratings are out of 5 and are consistent across every page on this site.
CriterionWeightWhat we measure
Payout speed30%Measured time from withdrawal request to funds available, split into approval and settlement, across multiple rails and times of day. Operator claims are ignored.
Bonus terms25%Wagering multiple and what it applies to, maximum bet during wagering, game weighting, expiry window, maximum conversion. An unpublished wagering requirement scores zero, not neutral.
NZD and New Zealand banking15%Whether a true NZD balance is available, which local methods actually work in the cashier, and what currency conversion costs if the balance is held in another currency.
Game range and studio quality15%Live catalogue counted rather than the marketing figure, which studios are present, and the average deployed RTP across a 60-title sample.
Support10%Response time and answer quality on a specific question that requires a specific answer.
Transparency and licensing5%Whether the licence number resolves on the regulator's register, whether a legal entity is named, and whether caps and fees are findable without contacting support.

Hard gates, and how to check if a casino is licensed NZ side

Some things are not weighted. They are pass or fail, and a failure caps where an operator can rank regardless of everything else.

  • An unverifiable licence caps an operator below first place. MadCasino scores well on offer size and would otherwise rank higher; it does not publish its licence, so it cannot top a ranking on this site.
  • An unpublished wagering requirement scores zero on the bonus criterion. Not an estimated value — zero. If a reader cannot price the offer, neither can we, and we will not guess on an operator's behalf.
  • A refused legitimate withdrawal removes an operator entirely. No score, no listing, no exceptions.
  • No responsible gambling tools removes an operator entirely. Deposit limits, loss limits, time-outs and self-exclusion must exist in account settings.
  • Any marketing that could target under-18s removes an operator entirely.

How we make money, stated plainly

RNRV earns affiliate commission when a reader opens an account through a link on this site. That is the entire business model. We would rather say it in a heading than bury it in a footer.

What that does not buy

  • Placement. No operator has paid for a position in any ranking on this site.
  • Pre-publication review. No operator sees a page before it goes live.
  • Editorial input. No operator has suggested, amended or approved any wording here.
  • Protection from criticism. Two operators we are paid by carry published warnings in every table they appear in.

The test we apply to ourselves

Does commission predict ranking?

It should not, and it does not. Our two lowest-paying operators are Spino (25%) and the TechOptions group sites including IviBet, HellSpin and SlotsGem (20%). Spino ranks second on our fast payout page and carries the only 0x-wagering offer we recommend.

Meanwhile MadCasino pays a mid-tier 45% and is ranked third overall with a licence warning attached, and CrownSlots and Gunsbet pay our highest rate at 50% — CrownSlots tops our homepage ranking on merit, and we say plainly on our fast payout page that its approval step is the slowest of any site in our top five.

If you think a ranking looks commercially convenient, tell us. That challenge is the most useful thing a reader can send.

What we also recommend that pays us nothing

On online betting and sports betting sites, we recommend TAB NZ — which pays us nothing — because it is the only operator legally permitted to take a New Zealand bet and the only one offering New Zealand consumer protection. We put that recommendation above the affiliate links rather than below them.

What we refuse to do

Never on this site

  • Paid placement or "sponsored" entries dressed as rankings
  • Operator-written or operator-approved copy
  • Fabricated user reviews or invented testimonials
  • Countdown timers, fake scarcity or manufactured urgency
  • Claiming to have tested something we have not
  • Publishing a bonus figure we cannot verify in the operator's own terms
  • Omitting a material negative because the operator pays well
  • Any content designed to appeal to under-18s

Always on this site

  • Affiliate disclosure on every page carrying a link
  • A named author and a review date on every page
  • Measured figures labelled as measured, and estimates labelled as estimates
  • "Check current terms" where we could not verify, instead of a guess
  • Negative findings published alongside positive ones
  • Primary sources cited for every legal and tax claim
  • Responsible gambling information on every page
  • Corrections published with their date

Editorial standards

Named authorship and expertise

Every page is written by one of three named reviewers, each with a defined specialism and relevant background. Credentials are on the authors page. We do not publish anonymous content.

Separation of fact and assessment

Where we state something as measured — a withdrawal time, an overround, an RTP — it was measured by us and we say when. Where we are giving a view, it reads as a view. Where an operator's claim is unverified, we attribute it to the operator rather than adopting it.

Primary sources for legal and tax claims

Every legal, regulatory and tax statement on this site is traceable to legislation, a government publication or published professional analysis, cited on the page. Where the Department of Internal Affairs or Inland Revenue is the authoritative source, we say so and link to it.

Dated review cycles

Bonus terms monthly. Payout timings quarterly, with fresh withdrawals. Legal pages monthly while the licensing regime is in transition. Every page shows its last review date and reviewer.

Responsible gambling is not a footer afterthought

Every page carries helpline information. Our rankings deduct for operators lacking deposit limits, loss limits, time-outs and self-exclusion. We publish the arithmetic showing that expected value is negative, including on pages where that is commercially inconvenient — the RTP comparison and the no-deposit valuation are both examples.

Corrections, complaints and challenges

If we have got something wrong

Tell us through the contact page. We correct the page, note the correction and its date at the foot, and update the review date. We do not silently edit.

The most useful reader reports are about things that change between our test cycles: a withdrawal that took far longer than our published figure, a bonus term that has been amended, a payment method that has stopped working. Those surface changes we would otherwise catch a quarter late.

If you have a dispute with an operator

We cannot intervene on your behalf — we are a comparison site, not a regulator or an ombudsman, and we have no authority over any operator. What we can do is point you at the escalation path that works, which is set out in full on our complaints section. And we do want to hear about it, because a pattern of credible unresolved complaints is grounds for removing an operator from this site.

If you are an operator

You are welcome to correct a factual error, and we will fix it and note the correction. You cannot review a page before publication, request a ranking change, or purchase placement. Contact details are on the contact page.
