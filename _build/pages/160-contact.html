What we cannot do

Said plainly, so nobody waits on a reply that was never going to help.

  • We cannot make an operator pay you. We have no authority over any casino or bookmaker. The escalation path that does work is in our complaints section.
  • We cannot give individual gambling advice. No recommendations tailored to your circumstances, no views on staking, and no tips.
  • We cannot access, close or recover your casino account. That is between you and the operator.
  • We cannot give legal or tax advice. Our law and tax pages are information with primary sources cited. For your own circumstances, speak to a New Zealand lawyer or chartered accountant.
  • We cannot provide counselling or crisis support. The services at the top of this page can, free and confidentially.

Our corrections policy

If we publish something wrong, we fix it, note the correction and its date at the foot of the page, and update the review date. We do not silently edit a page and leave the date unchanged, because a review date that does not mean anything is worse than none.

Material corrections — a payout figure, a legal claim, a bonus term that changes a recommendation — are noted on the page itself. Minor fixes such as typographical errors are made without a note.

Our full editorial standards, scoring weights and conflicts policy are on the how we review page, and the people behind the bylines are on the authors page.
