The short version

  • We collect very little: what you send us by email, and anonymous analytics about how the site is used.
  • We do not sell personal information to anyone, ever.
  • We do not collect gambling account details, payment details, or information about your play.
  • When you click through to an operator, that operator's own privacy policy applies — not ours.
  • You can ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it: privacy@rnrv.co.nz.

1. Who we are

RNRV operates the website at rnrv.co.nz, an independent New Zealand information and comparison publication about online casinos, pokies and betting sites. We are the agency responsible for personal information collected through this site for the purposes of the Privacy Act 2020.

Privacy contact: privacy@rnrv.co.nz

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Email correspondence. If you contact us — a correction, a reader report about a withdrawal, a question — we receive your email address, your name if you give it, and whatever you write. We keep it while we are dealing with it and for a reasonable period afterwards.
  • Details in a reader report. If you tell us about a slow withdrawal or a changed bonus term, we may retain the operational details (operator, dates, amounts as ranges) to compare against our testing log. We do not publish your identity and we do not need your account number — please do not send one.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics data. Which pages are viewed, approximate region (country or city level), referring page, device type, browser, and time on page. This is aggregated and used to understand which content is useful.
  • Technical data. IP address, user agent and request logs, recorded by our hosting provider for security and diagnostic purposes.
  • Affiliate click tracking. When you click a link to an operator, the click is recorded so that commission can be attributed. This uses an identifier assigned to the click, not your name.

What we never collect

We do not collect, hold, request or have access to: your gambling account credentials; your payment card, bank or cryptocurrency details; your deposit, withdrawal or betting history; your identity documents; or any information about your play at any operator. If a page or message purporting to be from RNRV asks for any of those, it is not from us — please report it to privacy@rnrv.co.nz.

4. Cookies and tracking

This site uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies. Full detail, including how to control or refuse them, is in our cookie policy.

In summary: strictly necessary cookies for the site to function; analytics cookies to understand usage; and affiliate tracking identifiers set when you click through to an operator. We do not use advertising cookies for behavioural retargeting.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We have never sold it and we will not.

We share limited information with service providers who help us operate the site:

  • Hosting provider — serves the site and retains technical logs.
  • Analytics provider — processes aggregated usage data.
  • Email provider — handles our correspondence.
  • Affiliate networks and operators — receive click attribution data so commission can be assigned. This does not include your name or email address.

We may also disclose information where required by New Zealand law, or where necessary to protect our legal rights, prevent fraud, or address a security incident.

6. What happens when you click through to an operator

Important

When you click a link from this site to a casino or betting operator, you leave rnrv.co.nz and that operator's privacy policy applies, not ours. Anything you then provide — your name, address, date of birth, identity documents, payment details — goes to that operator, not to us. We never see it.

Those operators are licensed offshore and may hold and process your information outside New Zealand under different privacy laws. Read their privacy policy before you register. We have no control over and no responsibility for their practices.

7. Storage and overseas disclosure

Information collected through this site may be stored or processed on servers located outside New Zealand, including by our hosting, analytics and email providers.

Where personal information is disclosed to an overseas recipient, we take reasonable steps to ensure that recipient is required to protect it with safeguards comparable to those in the Privacy Act 2020, consistent with information privacy principle 12.

9. Your rights under the Privacy Act 2020

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of information you believe is inaccurate, and to have a statement of correction attached if we do not amend it.
  • Request deletion of information where we no longer have a proper basis for holding it.
  • Withdraw consent to non-essential cookies at any time — see the cookie policy.
  • Complain to us, and to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner if you are not satisfied with our response.

How to make a request

Email privacy@rnrv.co.nz describing what you want. We may need to verify your identity before releasing information, which for an email-based request usually means replying from the address the correspondence came from.

We will respond as soon as reasonably practicable and in any case within 20 working days, as required by the Privacy Act 2020.

If you are not satisfied

You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner: privacy.org.nz, or 0800 803 909. We would ask that you raise it with us first so we have a chance to put it right.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, use, modification or disclosure. The site is served over HTTPS, access to correspondence is limited to the editorial team, and we hold as little personal information as possible — which is itself the most effective protection available.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a privacy breach that has caused or is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner as required by the Privacy Act 2020.

11. Children

This site is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has provided us with personal information, contact privacy@rnrv.co.nz and we will delete it.

Parents and guardians who wish to block gambling-related sites at device level can use free tools such as BetBlocker, or the parental controls built into iOS, Android, Windows and macOS. See our responsible gambling page.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always the one on this page and the "last updated" date shows when it last changed. Material changes will be noted at the top of the page.

13. Contact

Privacy enquiries and requests: privacy@rnrv.co.nz
All other enquiries: editor@rnrv.co.nz or via our contact page.
