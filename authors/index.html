How authorship works here

Every page is signed

A named author, their role, and the date the page was last reviewed. No anonymous content, no operator-supplied copy, no house byline hiding who did the work.

Every page is checked

By someone other than the author. Legal claims go to Ana, payout claims to Tane, odds to Hemi — regardless of who wrote the page. Both names appear at the foot.

Specialisms are real

Hemi does not write about pokie RTP and Tane does not write about the Racing Industry Amendment Act. Where a page needs both, it names both.

Dates are honest

The review date at the foot of a page is when someone actually re-checked it, not when the file was last touched. If a page has not been reviewed recently, you can see that.

Corrections are attributed

When we get something wrong we fix it, note the correction and its date at the foot of the page, and update the review date. We do not silently edit.

Conflicts are declared

RNRV earns affiliate commission, disclosed on every page carrying a link. No author holds a personal financial interest in any operator beyond that site-wide relationship.

Why this page exists

Search engines call it E-E-A-T — experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trustworthiness — and it is a reasonable framework, but the underlying point is older and simpler than the acronym.

If a page tells you a casino paid a withdrawal in two hours, that claim is worth something only if you know who tested it, when, on what rail, and whether they had any reason to overstate it. If a page tells you the wagering requirement is 40x, it is worth something only if someone actually opened the terms. And if a page tells you what the Online Casino Gambling Act 2026 requires, it is worth something only if the person writing it can read legislation.

Most casino affiliate content answers none of those questions, because most of it is assembled from operator marketing by people who have never opened an account. This page, the methodology page and the byline on every article are how we make it possible for you to check us.

Including, we hope, checking us when we are wrong. The contact page goes to real people.

Frequently asked questions

Why does RNRV publish author names?

Because an unsigned review is an advertisement. If a page tells you a casino paid in two hours, you are entitled to know who requested that withdrawal, when, and what qualifies them to interpret the result.

Named authorship also creates accountability inside the team. A reviewer who has to attach their name to a payout claim checks it more carefully than one who does not.

Are these real people?

Yes. Each author covers a defined area, does the testing described on their profile, and is accountable for the accuracy of the pages they sign. Contact details for reaching the editorial desk, including for corrections addressed to a specific author, are on the contact page.

Do the authors gamble themselves?

Yes, necessarily — the testing requires real accounts, real deposits and real withdrawals. Every account on this site was funded with the reviewer's own money.

We treat that as a conflict to manage rather than ignore. Reviewers operate to a fixed testing budget per site, do not chase losses as part of testing, and do not hold a personal financial interest in any operator beyond the affiliate relationship declared site-wide. Testing spend is recorded in the same log as the results.

Can I contact an author directly?

Through the contact page, addressed to the author by name. Corrections, challenges to a ranking, and reports of a withdrawal experience that differs from our published figures all reach the relevant person.

We cannot provide personal gambling advice, act on a dispute with an operator, or recommend a site for your individual circumstances.

Who fact-checks the content?

Every page is checked by a person other than its author before publication. Legal, regulatory and tax claims are checked by Ana Whitaker against primary sources regardless of who wrote the page. Payout and payment claims are checked against the testing log by Tane Rāwiri. Odds and margin figures are checked by Hemi Toka.

The reviewer and the fact-checker are named at the foot of each page.
