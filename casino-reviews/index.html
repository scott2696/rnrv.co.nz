Frequently asked questions

How does RNRV review a casino?

A reviewer opens an account in their own name from a New Zealand connection, completes KYC, deposits their own money on at least two payment rails, plays a minimum of 200 real-money spins or 100 live hands, requests a withdrawal and times it to the minute. Our editor separately reads the complete terms and conditions. Someone else tests live chat with a specific question.

About three weeks per operator. The full process and the scoring weights are on our how we review page.

Why do you review sites you criticise?

Because leaving them out helps nobody. MadCasino has the largest headline bonus available to New Zealanders and readers search for it constantly. We can decline to cover it and leave people to find it via a site that will not mention the unpublished licence — or cover it, score it honestly, and put the warning in every table it appears in.

We do the second. The same applies to Roby, whose licence is not clearly published and whose withdrawals took three days in our testing.

Are the ratings comparable between reviews?

Yes. Every operator is scored against the same six weighted criteria — payout speed 30%, bonus terms 25%, NZD banking 15%, game range 15%, support 10%, transparency 5% — so a 4.5 means the same thing on every page of this site.

Ordering differs by page because different pages rank on different things: our fast payout page orders on measured withdrawal time, our high payout page on catalogue RTP. The underlying rating does not change.

How often are reviews updated?

Bonus terms are re-verified monthly. Payout timings are re-measured quarterly with fresh withdrawals. Each review shows the date it was last reviewed and by whom at the foot of the page.

Reader reports are our other input — if your withdrawal took materially longer than our published figure, tell us and we will re-test ahead of schedule.

Do operators pay to be reviewed?

No. No operator has paid for placement, seen a review before publication, or had any editorial input. We earn affiliate commission when a reader opens an account through our links, which is disclosed on every page.

Our two highest-commission operators are CrownSlots and Gunsbet at 50%; our lowest are the TechOptions group sites at 20%. If commission drove the rankings, Spino at 25% would not rank second for payout speed and MadCasino at 45% would not be listed third with a warning. The full position is on our methodology page.
