Cookie Policy

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on rnrv.co.nz, what each does, how long it lasts and how to turn it off. We use very few, we do not run behavioural advertising, and nothing here is required for you to read the site.

The short version

  • We use a small number of cookies: strictly necessary ones, anonymous analytics, and affiliate click attribution.
  • We do not run behavioural advertising or retargeting, and we do not sell data to advertisers.
  • You can refuse or delete any non-essential cookie at any time and the site will still work.
  • Operators you click through to set their own cookies under their own policies — not ours.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store. When you return, the browser sends it back, which lets the site recognise the same browser. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels, and this policy covers those too.

Cookies are either first-party (set by rnrv.co.nz) or third-party (set by another domain, such as an analytics or affiliate provider). They are either session cookies, which are deleted when you close the browser, or persistent cookies, which last a defined period.

Strictly necessary

These keep the site working and secure, and record the cookie choices you make so we do not ask again on every page. They hold no personal information beyond what is needed for that, and cannot be switched off without breaking the site.

Analytics

Aggregated data about which pages are read, for how long, from roughly which region, and on what device type. We use this to decide which content to expand and which to retire. IP addresses are truncated or anonymised where the provider supports it, and we do not attempt to identify individual readers.

Affiliate attribution

When you click a link to a casino or betting operator, that operator's affiliate network may set a cookie recording that the click came from RNRV. If you later open an account, the network attributes commission to us. This is how the site is funded and we disclose it on every page carrying such a link.

That cookie is set by the affiliate network or the operator — not by us — and is governed by their privacy policy. Refusing it does not change your experience on the operator's site; it means we are not credited for the referral.

3. Cookies set by operators you visit

When you click through to a casino or betting operator, you leave this site. That operator sets its own cookies and applies its own cookie and privacy policies, which we neither control nor have visibility of. Those operators are licensed offshore and may use tracking we would not use.

Read the operator's own cookie policy if this matters to you, and use the browser controls below to manage their cookies as you would any other site's.

4. How to control cookies

On this site

You can accept or refuse non-essential cookies through the notice presented on your first visit, and change your choice later by clearing this site's cookies in your browser and reloading the page. Refusing analytics and affiliate cookies has no effect on your ability to read anything here.

In your browser

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, either globally or per site. Blocking all cookies may affect other websites you use.

Private browsing

Private or incognito mode discards cookies when the window closes. It does not make you anonymous to websites or to your internet provider, but it is an effective way to read this site leaving nothing behind.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We honour Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals where the browser sends them, treating a GPC signal as a refusal of non-essential cookies.

5. What we do not do

  • No behavioural advertising. We do not run ad networks, retargeting pixels or personalised ad tracking on this site.
  • No selling of data. We do not sell or rent personal information or cookie data to anyone.
  • No cross-site profile building. We do not build profiles of readers across other websites.
  • No social media tracking pixels.

The site is funded by affiliate commission, which is why affiliate attribution cookies are the one commercial technology we use, and why we describe them plainly rather than as "partner cookies".

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The version on this page is always current and the "last updated" date shows when it last changed. If we ever introduced a materially different category of cookie, we would say so at the top of this page and ask for consent again.

7. Contact

Questions about cookies or privacy: privacy@rnrv.co.nz. Full contact details on our contact page.

If you are not satisfied with our response you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz or 0800 803 909.
