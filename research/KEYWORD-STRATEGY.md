## 6. What we deliberately did *not* target

- **"no KYC casino NZ"** and **"instant withdrawal no verification"** — high volume, but we would have to recommend operators that skip AML obligations. We answer the queries and explain why the answer is no, rather than building a page that ranks by recommending it.
- **"casino not on GamStop"** — a UK concept with no NZ application. Chasing it would attract the wrong audience.
- **Aggressive bonus-code pages** — low durability, and Part 4 of the 2026 Regulations makes inducement-led advertising the most exposed category.
- **Free-play / social casino** — different intent, and it would dilute the money-page clusters.
